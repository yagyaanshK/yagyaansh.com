Purpose

Build a rig that runs its own physics experiments. It:

  1. observes a servo-driven pendulum with a webcam;
  2. estimates the pendulum's state despite latency;
  3. chooses its own excitation experiments, so that it identifies the system's physics as fast as possible;
  4. uses the identified model for feedback control.

Every claim is backed by a measurement against an independent reference.

Hardware baseline (owned parts only, as decided on 2026-09-26)

In use:

  • Board: ELEGOO UNO R3 or the Mega 2560 clone (5 V logic).
  • Servo: SG90 (plastic gears).
  • Camera: the laptop webcam. Measured: 640×480 at about 30 fps, manual exposure works, fixed focus.
  • Pivot: an improvised pin through a hole in the rod. There is no bearing, so Coulomb friction is expected, and the model includes it.
  • Timing reference: a photoresistor light gate.

Upgrades, bought only if the go/no-go gate shows they are needed:

  • U1: AS5600 magnetic angle sensor (a direct angle reference).
  • U2: MG996R metal-gear servo.
  • U3: 608 bearing.
  • U4: a 5 V ≥3 A supply.
  • U5: an external 30–60 fps webcam.

Requirements

Each requirement has an ID, a measurable criterion, and the method used to verify it.

Sensing (S)

ID Requirement Verification
R-S1 The camera delivers ≥ 28 fps at 640×480 with manual exposure ≤ 8 ms. E0: frame-interval statistics over ≥ 60 s
R-S2 Markers are detected in ≥ 98% of delivered frames under the rig lighting. E1: detection rate over ≥ 60 s of swinging
R-S3 Static angle accuracy vs a printed protractor reference: RMS error ≤ 1.0° over 7 angles in [−45°, 45°]. E1a
R-S4 Camera-derived swing period agrees with the light-gate period within 0.2%. Constant delays cancel in a period, so this checks timing consistency. E1b
R-S5 End-to-end camera latency (from an LED toggled by the board to the frame showing it) is measured as a distribution: median, IQR, resolution ≤ ±17 ms. Go/no-go: median ≤ 150 ms. E1c
R-S6 Board-to-host clock offset is estimated by ping round-trip, with uncertainty ≤ 2 ms. E1c

Actuation (A)

ID Requirement Verification
R-A1 Servo commands are sent at 50 Hz. Commands are software-limited to |φ_cmd| ≤ 60°. Code inspection + unit test
R-A2 The servo is powered from a supply separate from the board's 5 V pin, with a shared ground. There are no board resets during a 10-minute excitation test. E0 power test, and an event-log check for resets
R-A3 Servo dead time, natural frequency and rate limit are identified, with uncertainty reported. E3

Hypotheses (to be tested, not assumed)

A negative result, reported with its evidence, is an acceptable outcome.

  • H1 (identification efficiency), v1.1, pre-registered for hardware: Bayesian D-optimal excitation reaches a held-out 3 s prediction NRMSE within 10% of the noise floor in ≤ 50% of the experiment time the chirp baseline needs to reach the same level. The noise floor is the NRMSE of the best available model on the same held-out data: the true parameters in simulation, and a long-record fit on hardware. Tested in simulation and on hardware (≥ 8 runs per method, interleaved).
    • ~~v1.0: D-optimal reaches the chirp's held-out 3 s NRMSE at the full budget in ≤ 50% of the experiment time.~~ This was mis-specified: at the full budget the chirp is already at the noise floor (1.003×), so the comparison mostly measures noise.
  • H2 (actuator and latency modelling): Including the servo model and latency compensation reduces closed-loop RMS amplitude error by ≥ 30% compared with the same controller without them.
  • H3 (damping law): The damping law is identifiable. Viscous + Coulomb and viscous + quadratic are distinguishable by held-out negative log-likelihood (ΔNLL significant across runs).

Go/no-go gate (2026-10-05)

Continue with this hardware if all three hold:

  1. R-S3 is met (angle RMS ≤ 1.0°).
  2. The median latency in R-S5 is ≤ 150 ms.
  3. v1.1: In simulation, the control-relevant parameters (κ and the servo's combined phase delay) are recovered within 5% on ≥ 90% of seeds. Damping parameters are reported with their uncertainties but are not gated. ~~v1.0: R-I1 is met in simulation.~~

Otherwise:

  • If (1) fails: buy U1 (AS5600).
  • If (2) fails: buy U5 (external webcam).
  • If the servo or pivot mechanics fail: buy U2 and U3.
  • If the rig as a whole is not viable: switch to the fallback project (see ../PROJECT_STRATEGY.md).
